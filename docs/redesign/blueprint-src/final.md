# Sehari Semalam: one Jakarta day, lit by the Logink ribbon

**Recommended direction (key: `final`).** dir-journey 'Sehari Semalam: 360° in 24 hours' (winner by total, 21.0: brand 6, conversion 7, build 8), rebuilt with the must-fix items from all three judges and grafts from dir-night (20.5) and dir-day (20.5).

Specimen: `scratchpad/final/spec.html`, rendered as `spec-1440.jpg` and `spec-390.jpg`. Contrast script: `scratchpad/final/cr.mjs`. Structured source: `scratchpad/final/final.json`.

## Thesis

'360°' is one full turn of the sun, and an Indonesian customer's path to a business runs across one day. They search at 07.00, check Instagram and reviews at lunch, ask an AI assistant at dusk and tap WhatsApp at night. Sehari Semalam tells the homepage as one Jakarta day in one place: a ruko street with a coral-awning kedai and the rooftop above it. The fixed logo supplies all of the colour. The ribbon's four stops (#A8138F → #D81C5C → #EE3D5E → #F88438) are the day's four lights and the four stages of the loop in aeo-geo §4.1: Ditemukan at 07.00 in magenta, Dipercaya at 12.40 in crimson, Dikutip AI at 18.05 in coral, where the whole ribbon appears once as the sunset, and Dihubungi at 21.30 in the orange of sodium streetlight, ending on WhatsApp green. By day the site is bright newsprint paper. That keeps it readable outdoors on a 4G Android and continuous with the light brand shipped two months ago. At dusk it turns plum and at night indigo, the two hues that bracket the wordmark's violet 'ink'. At the next dawn it is paper again, because the loop repeats. The voice is the Indonesian koran pagi and koran sore: condensed-caps headlines, and datelines ('JAKARTA, 07.00 WIB —') that double as answer-first ledes. Everything a reader or crawler needs (keyword H1s, services, prices, FAQ answers, proof) is server HTML on flat surfaces. The film, the rooftop time-lapse canvas, the cursor and the parallax are desktop layers, each with a CSS or poster version on phones. It fits Logink because it turns '360° with AEO and GEO' into one day anyone recognises, spends colour only where the logo already does, and makes the new AI stage the brand's single bold moment.

STRUCTURAL RULE
Every text node sits on one of six flat surfaces (Putih, Kertas, Senja, Senja Dalam, Malam, Malam Dalam), or inside a Semburat highlight. Nothing sits on photos, film, canvas or the horizon band. CI therefore tests AA as a token × surface matrix, including pinned states, and a lint rule blocks the banned pairs above. The ribbon horizon and the house-lights spill are text-free by construction. prefers-contrast: more swaps Aspal 70 and Cahaya 70 for full-strength ink and Cahaya. forced-colors gets a plain underline instead of the Stabilo highlight.

Why this type: the drama comes from the width axis of one grotesque, not from a serif. Ultra-condensed heavy capitals are the Indonesian koran headline. Against them sit one expanded light line (the brand judge's 'Kata Lebar' graft) and thin condensed numerals. The rendered specimen confirms that the home H1, lede and CTA pair fit the first 844px at 390px with no overflow, and that 'Jasa Pembuatan Website & Landing Page Jakarta' stays at display size in three lines (scratchpad/final/spec-1440.jpg, spec-390.jpg, spec.html). The judge's Anybody fallback is not needed, because wdth 125 already reads as a deliberate switch.

Budget: two families and one preloaded file. The total is 100,472 B (98.1 KiB), within ≤100 KiB and ≤2 preloads. If the budget is read in decimal kB, Martian Mono is dropped for the system mono stack at 0 B.

CLS guard for the condensed display:
- Each display line is its own span with white-space: nowrap and a fixed line-height, so a font swap can change width but never the line count. The title container uses overflow-x: clip.
- adjustFontFallback is replaced by a hand-tuned fallback: local('Roboto Condensed'), local('Arial Narrow'), then Arial with size-adjust.
- CLS is measured at 390 and 1440 on throttled 4G.
- Escape hatch: a second @font-face alias of the same file, with font-display: optional, used for display lines only.

Rules:
- Never define --font-sans. Logo.tsx renders its wordmark through var(--font-sans, -apple-system, system-ui), so defining it would change the fixed logo.
- The latin file lacks U+2192 (→) and U+2264 (≤), verified in its cmap, so arrows are inline SVG and visible copy avoids ≤.
- Banned faces: Bricolage, Outfit, Space Grotesk, Manrope, Inter, Geist, and any serif.
- There is no gradient text and no underlight.

## Film

'Buka', the hero at 05.30 Fajar. It is a single locked-off shot, 8s at 24fps, with the first frame equal to the last (xfade seam via encode-webm.sh).

SHOT
- A quiet ruko street in Jakarta Timur at first light, the same street as the canvas's kedai.
- Overnight-rain puddles reflect a pale pink-lilac sky, and the sodium street lamps are still on.
- Small in frame right, back half-turned, a woman in her 40s in a sage hijab arranges jars of kerupuk in her half-open kedai under a coral awning. The awning is the one ribbon-colour object.
- A kettle steams, and one unbranded scooter crosses far off on the left side of the road.
- The street lamp clicks off at 5s. At 6s the face-down phone on the counter glows at its edges, foreshadowing 21.30. Screens are never readable.

PRODUCTION
- Figma Weave: Nano Banana Pro still, then Veo 3.x image-to-video (Kling as the alternate). Locked camera, push-in ≤3%, no face close-ups, no speech.
- Graded with the Logink day preset: neutral whites, shadows toward plum h≈323, greens −20.

DELIVERY
- Desktop: 1920×820 (21:9) VP9, crf ~33, ≤1.5 MB. It plays full-bleed under the headline like a front-page photo.
- Poster: WebP 1280w, ≤80 KB, fetchpriority high, BT.709-matched so the swap is invisible.
- Markup: <video muted playsinline loop preload='none' aria-hidden>. The src is attached only after window load, only in view, and never under Save-Data, reduced motion or Mode teks. It pauses offscreen and on visibilitychange.
- Phones get no video: a 4:3 poster ≤60 KB with a CSS 12s push-in (scale 1 → 1.04, transform only).
- No VideoObject.

CAPTION AND FALLBACK
- The caption line under the film is the disclosure: 'Foto/film: ilustrasi AI · bukan klien Logink'.
- If the AI video fails QA on hands, faces, banding or the loop seam, ship the graded still with a CSS light sweep. Nothing else depends on the film.
- ffmpeg must be installed and a real dawn clip encode-tested for shadow blocking before the art direction is locked.

## Scroll-scrubbed canvas sequence

'Satu atap, satu hari', the Overture on home.

WHAT IT DEPICTS
A locked-off 16:9 view from a fifth-floor rooftop over a ruko neighbourhood in Tebet, Jakarta Selatan: orange and blue toren water tanks, satellite dishes, clotheslines with batik shirts, a mosque dome and minaret, a curving elevated toll road, and towers fading into haze. At street level sits the coral-awning kedai from the film. Time runs 07.00 → 12.40 → 18.05 → 21.30: shadows sweep and shorten, the haze whitens at noon, the sky sets through coral and magenta, about 70% of windows light up, and headlights become streaks.

PRODUCTION (primary path: a deterministic 2.5D composite, per the build judge's graft)
- Plates (Figma Weave, Nano Banana Pro): one day base plate with the sky masked; one night relight of the same plate (a single image edit, checked for ≤2px silhouette drift); four sky-only plates; a window-light mask; a haze layer; a traffic-streak sprite path along the toll road.
- Claude Design assembles them into one HTML scene where every layer is a pure function of --p. The sky crossfades, the day base dissolves into the night base between 18.05 and 21.30, the windows screen-blend in, the shadow and haze layers move, and the streaks run along the path.
- render-html-frames.cjs renders the scene and frames.sh converts it to WebP. No generative step has to hold geometry across frames. First/last-frame video interpolation is only an experiment, never on the schedule.
- The canvas holds no UI and no text, so one frame set serves ID and EN. The phone in front is live DOM.

BUDGET (render and measure before committing)
- 120 frames at 1280×720 WebP, q62–68, 40 frames per segment; the steps land on frames 0, 40, 80 and 119.
- Ship 120 only if the total is ≤2.5 MB. cinematic-tech measured about 28 KB/frame (3.36 MB) on a photographic clip.
- Otherwise drop to 96 frames, then q60, then 1120w. AVIF is about 10% smaller and is used only if the owner relaxes WebP-only.

TIERS (cinematic-tech ScrollSequence.tsx, fixed; the server snapshot is 'static')
- CANVAS: (pointer:fine) and (min-width:1024px); deviceMemory ≥4 or unknown (Safari counts as capable); not Save-Data; not reduced motion; not Mode teks. Desktop Firefox included. Frames load after window load, only within one viewport, coarse-to-fine (0, last, n/2 …), decoded with createImageBitmap inside a byte-budgeted window and released offscreen.
- LOOP: fine pointer with deviceMemory <4 gets an 8s VP9 loop, 1280w, ≤1 MB, over the poster.
- PHONES AND TABLETS: no canvas and no JS. Four keyframes at 600×800 WebP (about 35 KB each, lazy) sit in a sticky band 40svh tall (≤360px) and crossfade through CSS view-timelines tied to each step.
- STATIC (no JS, reduced motion, Save-Data, Firefox mobile, Googlebot): the SSR poster <img alt='Atap ruko Tebet, Jakarta Selatan, pukul 07.00'>, with each step's keyframe inline beside its text.

FIXES BEFORE USE
- Replace ScrollSequence's h-[250lvh] md:h-[350lvh] and h-lvh stage with a calc(4 × min(100svh, 900px)) runway and a min(100svh, 900px) sticky stage.
- Delete its 'mobile' frame tier.

## Risks

- Time-lapse production is the riskiest asset. The 2.5D composite removes geometry drift, but it still needs a clean sky mask, a night relight within 2px, four skies and designer days in Claude Design. Figma Weave is not linked and ffmpeg is not installed. The realistic total for all assets is 2–3 weeks, including the bake-off. The film is optional at launch: its fallback is a graded still.
- Canvas weight: a photographic time-lapse changes every pixel, so 120 frames may land near 3.4 MB. Frames are rendered and measured first. The pre-committed fallbacks are 96 frames, q60 and 1120w, in that order; the scrub gets steppier (about 50px of scroll per frame).
- Visitors may not decode the clock. Stage names and service names always lead, and times play a supporting role (the stage strip, the table, the menu). The clock appears as numerals only on the four home chapter openers.
- A brand change two months after Warm Canvas. Mitigation: the site stays light-first on paper, the logo is untouched, and the owner signs off on the specimen (scratchpad/final/spec-1440.jpg) before build. The pilot is one service page.
- Thin proof: 3 SEO-only cases with data defects, 0 logos, 1 unverified testimonial, and no team or founding facts. Every proof slot collapses to offer facts or 'Cara kami mengukur'; nothing is invented. The owner must supply a named reviewer, logos and a non-SEO case.
- Condensed caps and font-swap CLS on the H1. The guard is one verb per nowrap line, a size-adjusted condensed fallback and an optional alias. If CLS still exceeds 0.05 on throttled 4G, the display lines switch to font-display: optional.
- Crimson small text on Kertas is 4.53:1, the thinnest AA margin. A lint rule allows it only on Putih.
- AI-generated people can look uncanny or be read as deceptive. People stay anonymous and small in frame or mid-action, everything carries a caption label, and an asset manifest is kept (Anthropic AUP; UU 8/1999 Pasal 17).
- Dark dawn and dusk footage bands and blocks in 8-bit VP9. Encode-test before locking; the dawn film is lighter than night footage, and the static-noise trick is used on the ribbon band only.
- Firefox (3.9% in Indonesia) and pre-26 Safari get static versions of the view()-driven effects (dial dot, horizon wipes, phone keyframe crossfade). Desktop Firefox still gets the JS canvas.
- Lenis plus sticky plus canvas needs real-device QA: Windows wheels, the Safari 60fps cap, anchors under the nav, back/forward restoration, and the Maps iframe on Local SEO.
- Mode teks is a novel control and could confuse non-technical visitors. It stays inside Bab III and the footer and is usability-tested once.
- Night indigo plus WhatsApp green could drift toward a 'gaming' look. The guard is indigo with real chroma (not black), orange as the only night accent, green only on WhatsApp controls, and a neutral partner button.

## Why it wins

1. It keeps the highest-scoring idea (21/30, and the build judge's first choice). '360° = one day' is the most original and most Indonesian concept of the three. One kedai and one rooftop run through the film, the canvas and the stills, and the whole idea costs 0 KB of JS, because the chapter light is static CSS.

2. It fixes the idea's weak execution with the other two directions' best parts. That removes the brand judge's 'rainbow template' objection (five unrelated hue families down to neutral paper, plum and indigo). The typography moves from generic news-site Archivo Narrow to one Archivo file whose width axis gives film-poster condensed caps and the single wide 'Dikutip AI.'. The boldness sits in one place: the ribbon sunset at the AI stage, the service Logink most needs to own.

Phones get zero motion JS and a native version of every moment. 5. It is unoccupied ground. cmlabs is SaaS blue, crawlcompass is near-black and orange, Epilog is cream, serif and orange, narrativ is white and lime, egghead is orange and black. A Jakarta newspaper day lit by the Logink ribbon belongs to nobody.
